VVIP rule

Association is not causation.

Small counts, traffic levels, daylight, weather, advance publicity, multiple testing, alert duration and handset receipt timing complicate inference. A non-significant finding does not establish safety.

Independent replication

Source receipts before publication.

Research datasets, source hashes, full calculations and independent reviewer challenges remain controlled until a named human approves an exact public release.

Important limitation

STATS19 does not measure every road incident.

It covers police-reported personal-injury collisions. Damage-only bumps and near misses may be absent, so a null STATS19 finding does not prove that the alarm caused no driving disturbance.
