Historical evidence

The primary result is currently negative.

The pre-specified endpoint is police-reported personal-injury collisions from the archived alert time to 15 minutes later. Association is not causation.

2025 first five minutes

RR 1.74
5 observed · 2.88 control mean · empirical p 0.057

This was a pre-specified secondary sensitivity window, not a post-hoc replacement for the 0–<15 minute primary endpoint. Because several secondary windows are examined, the value is treated as exploratory, non-confirmatory and subject to multiplicity. It must not be promoted as evidence that alerts increase collisions.

The rule that governs this Observatory

Statistical signal ≠ causation. Discovery, sensitivity tests and public evidence are deliberately separated. A result only becomes public after provenance and publication checks.

Important limitation

STATS19 does not measure every road incident.

It covers police-reported personal-injury collisions. Damage-only bumps and near misses may be absent, so a null STATS19 finding does not prove that the alarm caused no driving disturbance.
