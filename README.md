# UK Emergency Alert Impact Observatory

Public, evidence-led companion to the private UK Emergency Alert Impact Research engine.

The Observatory publishes only validated, non-sensitive outputs. It does **not** treat statistical association as proof of causation and does not publish personal or identifiable safeguarding/health records.

## Current status

- 2023 national test: no positive collision excess detected on the pre-specified 0–<15 minute endpoint.
- 2025 national test: higher than matched controls on the 0–<15 minute endpoint, but not statistically persuasive; a 0–<5 minute elevation remains exploratory.
- 14 August 2026 wildfire alert: primary collision analysis awaiting suitable 2026 collision data.
