14 AUG 2026 · CONTEXT

Wildfire alert formed part of a wider emergency response

The Cabinet Office announcement linked the alert to severe wildfire conditions, emergency funding, military assistance and a temporary disposable-barbecue ban. These measures are contextual co-interventions and must be considered before attributing any later change in outcomes to the phone alert itself.

METHOD NOTE

Driving guidance is directly relevant to the road-safety hypothesis

Official Emergency Alerts guidance tells drivers and riders not to read or respond to an alert while moving and to find somewhere safe and legal to stop. The Observatory tests aggregate collision outcomes; it does not infer individual phone use.
